### World, Palette, and Style Rules:
- **Setting**: Satkhira, Bangladesh — muddy roads, tin roofs, kadam trees, paddy fields, shrimp gher ponds. Never a desert, never an Arab city.
- **Color Palettes**: 
    - Reality (Satkhira): wet green, muddy brown, tin-grey, soft monsoon light. 
    - Dream (নূর দ্বীপ / Noor Dwip): gold, turquoise, deep night blue, dramatic top-down light.
- **Sleep Transition**: Reality shifts to the dream island via a "dissolving into water waves" cut every single time.
- **Clothing**: Local Bangladeshi, 2020s. No Arab dress. Mahdi: panjabi + madrasah cap (cap OFF when playing). Girls: shalwar-kameez/frock + orna. Nana: lungi-panjabi with a gamcha on his shoulder.
- **Face Style**: Default to option "খ" (faceless: full body, clothing, posture, and hands shown, face left blank or softly blurred) or option "গ" (scene-centric: camera on hands/objects/silhouettes, not faces). Emotion is carried by posture and props. Do NOT default to fully-detailed drawn faces.
